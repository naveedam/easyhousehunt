How we keep listings trustworthy

  • Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, and every listing needs at least one photo.
  • If you change the price, address, photos or description of a live listing, it goes back for review.
  • Your conversations stay inside EasyHouseHunt, and we never sell your personal data.
  • If something looks wrong, tell us and we will look into it.

Renting safely

  • Visit the home in person before you pay anything.
  • Never pay a deposit or advance before you have seen the property and checked who owns it.
  • Be careful with prices that look too good to be true, or owners who cannot meet you.
  • Pay by traceable means and keep written proof of what you paid and why.

EasyHouseHunt is a platform that connects renters and owners. We are not a broker and not a party to any rental agreement.
